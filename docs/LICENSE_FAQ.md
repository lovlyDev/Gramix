# Licensing FAQ

English | [Русский](LICENSE_FAQ.ru.md)

**May I use Gramix, including at work or for commercial purposes?** Yes, under AGPL-3.0-only and the licenses of its third-party components.

**May I modify it and publish my own build?** Yes. Publish its source under AGPLv3, and do not present it as official Gramix; see [TRADEMARKS.md](../TRADEMARKS.md).

**May I host my own copy of the website?** Yes. If you changed it, section 13 of AGPLv3 requires you to offer its source to the site's users. The name and logo rules still apply.

**I need terms other than AGPL.** Contact me through GitHub. Any other permission for my own code exists only as a separate written agreement; it cannot change the licenses of third-party components.

**What license does release 0.1.0 have?** The same AGPL-3.0-only; see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

**What is Corresponding Source?** See sections 1, 5, 6, and 13 of [AGPLv3](../LICENSE) and the [source distribution notes](SOURCE_DISTRIBUTION.md).
